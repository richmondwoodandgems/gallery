Black walnut, cut long and shallow to keep the live edge and the band of pale
sapwood that runs the length of one side. The dark heartwood through the middle is
left plain so the grain rings carry the piece on their own.

Both ends are built up into clusters — clear quartz points, a banded agate eye,
yellow-green and amber stones, flecks of green — and then flooded with clear resin
so the surface finishes level with the wood.
